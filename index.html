Meet Nouaman

Your Surf Guide

Born where the Atlas meets the Atlantic, I caught my first wave at seven and never stopped. From local beginnings to coaching surfers from all over the world, the ocean shaped my path. Now back home, we share Morocco’s waves and culture with patient coaching, small groups, and a deep respect for nature.

Patient, Safety‑First Coaching

Clear, calm guidance in the water so you feel safe and confident on every wave.

Small Groups, Real Progress

We keep groups tight, watch every surfer, and give feedback that actually helps you improve.

Local Knowledge on Your Side

We choose the best spot and tide each day so you surf the right waves for your level.
